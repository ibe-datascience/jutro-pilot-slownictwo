Annotation worksheets

The two spreadsheets that wait for human judgement

Both files are meant to be downloaded, filled in and returned. They are shown here so that their content can be inspected without opening Excel.

  • Gold-set worksheet
  • Lemmatisation sheet (300 forms)

200 candidates drawn in strata (extraction method that ranked the candidate highest × frequency quartile × length). The sheet deliberately carries no model labels, so that two annotators can label independently. Filled in so far: annotator A 0, annotator B 0 of 200.

Source file: zloty_zbior_do_oznaczenia.xlsx (view · download).
