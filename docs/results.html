2 · Lemmatisation

Finding: the 4 best tools (Sonnet 5.5, Stanza + Morfeusz2, Haiku 5.5, spaCy + Morfeusz2) are 5 forms apart out of 300: 78.3% to 76.7%. That is less than the margin of error of one score (±4.6 points), so the benchmark does not rank them. The others are clearly lower: Morfeusz2, first reading 65.0%, spaCy 45.7%. The corpus is tagged with spaCy + Morfeusz2, which runs locally.
