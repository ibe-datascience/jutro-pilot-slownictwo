A subject candidate needs at least 3 occurrences in at least 2 biology materials, or at least 5 occurrences if it is found in a single material. A general candidate needs at least 5 occurrences in at least 3 materials of at least 3 other subjects. Keyness is computed with the true corpus sizes: 80 831 words of biology, 397 178 words of contrast, 81 664 894 words behind the KWJP100 list. For a multiword candidate PMI and logDice are computed for each pair of adjacent words and the lowest value is kept, so a phrase scores high only if every link in it is strong.

5 · LLM classification

Two models, sonnet-5-5 and opus-5-5, label every candidate independently. Each sees the candidate, its counts in biology and in the other subjects, and up to three sentences from the corpus; neither sees the rule-based pre-label or the other model’s answer. Where the two labels differ, or either model reports low confidence, fable-5-2 labels the candidate as well and the majority of the three decides. If all three differ, the adjudicator’s label is used and the row is marked for review.
