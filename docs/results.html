WarningWhat the reference is

The sheet has not been checked by a human yet. Until it is, the reference is the base form written by ZPE editors where one exists, and otherwise a provisional answer from the adjudicating language model. The editors’ forms were not written for this study (some keep the plural, for example), and a reference written by a language model may favour the LLM lemmatisers. The sheet itself is on the Annotation worksheets page.
