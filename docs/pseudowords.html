Pseudowords

Generated non-words for the LexTALE-style test: kept, rejected, and why

Templates are single real words from the general pool, stratified by length and syllable count. Each of the four generators proposes pseudowords for a template; filters then reject anything that is, or looks too much like, a real Polish word. imbir is a published ready-made list, sampled at random, so its rows are not derived from the template they sit next to.

WarningThe unipseudo rows of this run are not usable

The 240 kept unipseudo rows are copies of 3 strings (taliży, sensja, chcisy), repeated for every template. Step 7 starts the UniPseudo generator but does not read what it prints. It then takes the first rows of whichever CSV file lies in resources/pseudo/unipseudo/, and in this run that was the six-letter smoke-test file. The strings are therefore neither matched to their templates nor newly generated. Count 852 distinct pseudowords from the other three generators as the usable result of this run. The step needs fixing before the full run.
