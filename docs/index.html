JUTRO pilot: subject vocabulary and pseudowords

Biology · Polish primary school, grades 5–8 · ZPE teaching materials

ImportantStatus

Automatic candidate lists from a sample of 50 biology materials. Nothing has been checked by people yet.

Goal

A pupil with a migration background can fail a biology test because of weak Polish or because of missing biology concepts. The JUTRO test is meant to tell the two apart. This pilot builds the word material for it from the teaching materials of the ZPE platform.

What it produces

Output What it is for In this run
Subject pool biology terms, to test subject concepts 789 terms in 636 concepts
General pool everyday and school words, to test general Polish; each term gets a partner of similar frequency and length 3188 words; partner found for 514 terms (65%)
Pseudowords non-words for the word-recognition test (LexTALE type) 370 recommended, for 206 of 240 template words
Worksheets human check of the model labels and of the lemmatisation 400 + 300 rows, not filled in yet

Step Why How
1 · Corpus subject words stand out only against other subjects biology materials plus materials of all other subjects of the same school stage
2 · Tagging Polish inflects, so counting needs base forms spaCy + Morfeusz2
3 · Frequencies how common a word is in general Polish KWJP100 frequency list
4 · Candidates a complete, repeatable list before any model is involved every content word and noun phrase frequent enough in biology, scored by keyness and termhood measures
5 · Labels statistics cannot tell a biology term from a school word Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 label each candidate in its sentences; Fable 5.2 settles disagreements
6 · Pools terms and general words must be comparable T3 → subject pool, T1 and T2 → general pool; one-to-one matching on frequency and length
7 · Pseudowords non-words that pupils cannot spot by their shape five generators (Wuggy twice, UniPseudo, Opus 5.5, a published list) → hard filters → rating by Fable 5.2

Limits

  • Sample of 50 materials. Counts and rankings will change on the full corpus.
  • Labels and pseudoword ratings come from language models. Agreement between models is not accuracy.
  • A model reading 30 materials directly listed biology terms of which step 4 has 51%; the others are on the Pools page.
  • The filter for Ukrainian and Russian words works from word lists and does not replace review by bilingual staff.
