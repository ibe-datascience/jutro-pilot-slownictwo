JUTRO pilot: subject vocabulary and pseudowords

Biology · Polish primary school, grades 5–8 · ZPE teaching materials

ImportantStatus

Everything here is a candidate list produced by an automatic process on a sample of 50 biology materials (run probka50). No list has been validated by subject or language specialists, and no model label has been checked against human annotation yet.

Problem

Pupils with a migration background may fail a subject test for two different reasons: weak general Polish, or missing subject concepts despite adequate language. JUTRO aims to separate those barriers with three modules: recognition of general words against pseudowords, word associations, and matching subject terms to images.

This pilot builds the word material for biology (grades 5–8) from the teaching materials of the Integrated Educational Platform (ZPE): a pool of subject terms, a pool of general and school vocabulary, concept groups, and pseudowords for a LexTALE-style test.

Pipeline

One command runs eight steps. Each step reads the files written by the earlier ones, so a step can be re-run on its own, and every number on this site is read from a metrics file that a step saved.

python -m jutro run --config config/biologia_sp.yaml --probka 50   # sample of 50 target materials
python -m jutro run --config config/biologia_sp.yaml               # full corpus

1korpus Build the corpus
  • Select catalogue entries of school stage E2: materials of the target subject (Biologia) and, as contrast, of all other subjects. Teacher-facing material types and foreign-language subjects are dropped.
  • With --probka N: draw N target and 6·N contrast materials (seeded).
  • Parse the HTML into typed segments: title, body, definition, exercise, caption, image description.
  • Remove sentences repeated across many materials (platform templates), interface instructions and licence or metadata lines.
  • Estimate the grade of each target material from topic keywords.
  • Collect glossary entries and the editors' pairs inflected form → base form as silver standards.

2tagowanie Tag and lemmatise; compare tools

  • Target corpus again with Stanza, for comparison; speed test of the three tools on the same text.

  • Reference: the editors' base form where ZPE has one, otherwise a provisional answer from Opus 5.5; the sheet has columns for a human checker.

4kandydaci Extract and score term candidates
  • Single-word candidates: lemmas of content words known to Morfeusz2.
  • Multiword candidates: noun phrases of 2–4 words found by syntactic patterns (agreeing adjectives, genitive modifiers), rebuilt in the nominative.
  • Keep candidates with at least 3 occurrences in at least 2 target materials.
  • Score every candidate with every method: log-likelihood and log ratio against the contrast corpus; log-likelihood, log ratio and Simple Maths against KWJP100; TF-IDF across subjects; PMI and logDice for multiword candidates; TermoPL C-value.
  • Add a rule-based pre-label (T3?, T2?, T1? or undecided) from dispersion and frequency thresholds.
  • Compare each ranking with the silver standards (precision and recall at K).

5llm Classify candidates with language models
  • Select the top 1500 candidates of each of six rankings plus the most frequent widely dispersed words (250 each on a sample run).
  • Prompt per candidate: the form, its statistics and up to two sentences from the corpus.
  • Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 each return a category (T3 subject term, T2 school vocabulary, T1 everyday word, SZUM noise), a corrected base form, a concept label, imageability, confidence and a one-sentence reason.
  • Where the two labels differ or either confidence is low, Opus 5.5 decides.
  • Separately, Sonnet 5.5 lists terms directly from a sample of materials (saved; not used by later steps).
  • The cost of each batch of requests is estimated before it is sent; the run stops rather than pass the limit of 100 USD.

6pule Build the pools and the annotation worksheet
  • Join statistics with labels. The label used is the language-model label; candidates the models did not see keep the rule-based pre-label.
  • Where a model returned a different base form, it replaces the extracted one in forma_podstawowa; the extracted string stays in forma_kandydata.
  • T3 goes to the subject pool, T1 and T2 to the general pool.
  • For each subject term pick one unused general item with the same number of words and the closest Zipf frequency.
  • Group subject terms that share a concept label.
  • Draw 400 candidates (200 on a sample run) stratified by method, frequency and length into a worksheet for two human annotators; model labels are left out on purpose.

7pseudoslowa Generate and filter pseudowords
  • Templates: 240 single words from the general pool (80 on a sample run), spread over lengths and syllable counts.
  • Up to 6 pseudowords per template from each generator (4 on a sample run): Wuggy, UniPseudo, the language model (keeps the template's ending) and a random draw from the Imbir list.
  • Reject a pseudoword if it is known to Morfeusz2, occurs in the corpus or in wordfreq, sounds like a real word, is a productive prefix or suffix on an existing stem, or is one edit away from a real word.
  • Describe what is kept: length, syllables, consonant runs, diacritics, approximate OLD20, a coarse Ukrainian/Russian similarity flag.
  • Compare the distributions of templates and pseudowords.

Limits

  • This is a sample run. Counts and rankings will change on the full corpus.
  • The grade of a material is estimated from topic keywords; the ZPE catalogue only records the stage (grades 4–8).
  • Only 925 of 3513 candidates were labelled by the LLMs. The pools also contain candidates that carry only the rule-based pre-label; the label_source column on the data pages tells them apart.
  • For 122 candidates the language model returned a base form different from the extracted one, and for 60 of them with a different number of words. The statistics in those rows belong to the extracted string (forma_kandydata), and the same forma_podstawowa can appear in several rows of a pool. See the Candidates page.
  • The silver standard (ZPE glossaries) is incomplete, so precision@K against it is only useful for comparing methods.
  • SUBTLEX-PL is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and is used for comparison metrics only; it does not enter the published lists.
  • The UA/RU similarity flag on pseudowords compares against a built-in list of ten transliterated words. It is a placeholder for review by bilingual staff.
  • The unipseudo pseudowords of this run are not usable: the 240 rows are copies of 3 strings. See the note on the Pseudowords page.
